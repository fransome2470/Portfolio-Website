My practical expertise spans ISO 27001 implementation, SOC 2 Type 2 readiness and controls, GDPR compliance, risk management, and translating complex regulatory requirements into clear, business aligned frameworks that actually get used.

Beyond my day job, I contribute to open source GRC platforms and build free educational tools, because great security culture starts early. I'm actively open to collaboration, speaking opportunities, and volunteer cybersecurity teaching across the UK and beyond.
