🎯 Severity Thresholds & Response SLAs
● CRITICAL Score 17–25
Immediate escalation to CISO & Board. Treatment plan within 24 hours. Weekly tracking.
● HIGH Score 10–16
Escalate to Risk Owner. Treatment plan within 7 days. Bi-weekly review.
● MEDIUM Score 5–9
Assign to domain owner. Mitigation within 30 days. Monthly review.
● LOW Score 1–4
Document and monitor. Review quarterly. Accept if cost of mitigation exceeds exposure.
🔄 Risk Treatment Options
🛡️ Mitigate (Reduce)
Implement controls to reduce likelihood or impact. Primary option for Critical/High risks. Document control effectiveness and residual risk.
📋 Accept
Formally accept risk within risk appetite. Requires written sign-off from risk owner. Only applicable to Low/Medium risks.
🔀 Transfer
Transfer financial exposure via cyber insurance or contractual clauses. Does not eliminate the risk — residual risk remains.
🚫 Avoid
Eliminate the activity that generates the risk. Appropriate when the risk cannot be reduced to an acceptable level.
